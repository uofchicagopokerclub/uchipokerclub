# Product

## Who it is for

1. **UChicago students** deciding whether to come to a meeting. They need: when, where, whether beginners are
   welcome (yes), and how to join the mailing list.
2. **Members** checking the ledger and signing up for it with the code shared at meetings.
3. **The board**, recording results each week at `/ledger/record`.
4. **Recruiters and sponsors** from quant firms, who need a credible picture of the club and a way to ask for
   the sponsorship prospectus.

## What the site may say

- Facts come from the club's own material: the Fall 2026 info session deck, the 2026-2027 sponsorship
  prospectus, and the previous site. No invented numbers, quotes, logos, dates, or members.
- Stats carry their year (350+ on the mailing list in 2025-2026, about 40 in the 2025-2026 advanced cohort).
- Sponsors appear only as past sponsors with the disclaimer, until a new year's sponsors are confirmed.
- No sponsorship prices on the site. They live in the prospectus.
- The disclaimer "No money is wagered at any point. Chips are for scoring only." appears in the footer and on
  the ledger. Prize pools come from sponsor contributions (per the 2026-2027 prospectus), never from players.

## Data

- Emails collected by the sign-up and mailing-list forms are visible only to board members with access to the
  Sheet. The public feed carries names and results only.
- Names on the ledger are what members chose to show. First name and last initial is encouraged.
